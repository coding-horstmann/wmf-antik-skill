# Objects And Search Terms

Use compact three-part queries: a maker signal, one object class, and at most
one style or material signal. Keep broad, unbranded discovery in its own small
cap; it is a retrieval experiment, not maker evidence.

## Manufacturer And Design Seeds

- `WMF`, `W.M.F.`, `W M F`
- `Württembergische Metallwarenfabrik`, `Württembergische Metallwaren Fabrik`
- `Geislingen`
- `Ikora`, `Myra`
- `WMF I/O`, `WMF OX`, `WMF G` only as combined searches; never search an
  isolated one-letter mark token.
- `WMF Modell`, `WMF model number`, `WMF Entwurf`, `WMF design`

Treat designer names as a maintained, cited list rather than a permanent
hard-coded truth. A name appearing in a title is a discovery signal only.

## Broad Discovery Terms

Broad queries can catch poor titles but must be capped per source and tagged
with `discovery_scope: broad_unbranded`. Useful modifiers include `Nachlass`,
`Konvolut`, `lot`, `parti`, `dödsbo`, `samling`, `boedel`, and local estate or
collection terms. Do not treat a broad match as WMF evidence until photos or a
listing record provide it.

## Negative Terms

Exclude or sharply down-rank `Cromargan`, `Edelstahl`, `stainless`, `Besteckteil`,
`Ersatzteil`, `Reproduktion`, `repro`, `WMF style`, `im Stil von`, and their
local equivalents. Do not exclude a whole record merely because an adjacent
category term is present; verify the actual object and maker evidence first.
